# Guardrail · V/F argument-anchor dictionary (`guardrail.vf_anchor_dict`)

**Version: v1** · Binds `dec.bull` (V-anchors) and `dec.bear` (F-anchors). D13:
the bull/bear 论点锚词典 is a versioned catalog material — the two seats bind its
content digest. Revisions go through a proposal + human review (不设新管家); this
material replaces the bare legacy references to `playbook_V1_V10.md` /
`failure_modes_F1_F14.md`.

An anchored argument (one tagged with a V/F code AND carrying a cited number)
outranks bare rhetoric when the debate is judged. An assertion with no anchor and no
data support is down-weighted and may be named as such.

## Bull anchors (V1–V9) — used by `dec.bull`
- `V1` 业绩拐点 — earnings inflection / turnaround.
- `V2` 政策/订单催化 — policy tailwind or order-book catalyst.
- `V3` 估值低位修复 — valuation re-rating from a low percentile.
- `V4` 资金/北向流入 — capital / northbound inflow.
- `V5` 技术突破确认 — confirmed technical breakout.
- `V6` 行业景气上行 — improving industry prosperity.
- `V7` 供给收缩/涨价 — supply contraction / pricing power.
- `V8` 事件驱动(重组/回购) — event-driven (restructuring / buyback).
- `V9` 龙头溢价/护城河 — leader premium / durable moat.

## Bear anchors (F1–F14) — used by `dec.bear`
- `F1` 估值透支 — valuation overextended.
- `F2` 业绩证伪/爆雷 — earnings miss / blow-up.
- `F3` 商誉减值 — goodwill impairment.
- `F4` 解禁/减持压力 — lockup expiry / insider selling pressure.
- `F5` 质押爆仓风险 — share-pledge forced-liquidation risk.
- `F6` 现金流恶化 — deteriorating cash flow.
- `F7` 应收/存货异常 — receivables / inventory anomaly.
- `F8` 政策/监管收紧 — policy / regulatory tightening.
- `F9` 行业产能过剩 — industry overcapacity.
- `F10` 技术破位/趋势反转 — technical breakdown / trend reversal.
- `F11` 资金流出/北向减仓 — capital outflow / northbound reduction.
- `F12` 拥挤交易/炒板反指 — crowding / limit-up churn reversal signal.
- `F13` 治理/关联交易 — governance / related-party transaction risk.
- `F14` 立案调查/退市风险 — investigation / delisting risk.

## Discipline
- Anchors are a shared, versioned vocabulary — a seat cites an anchor, it does not
  redefine or invent one. Proposing a new/revised anchor is a human-reviewed change
  to this material, never an inline seat decision.
