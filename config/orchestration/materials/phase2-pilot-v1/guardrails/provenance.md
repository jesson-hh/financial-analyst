# Provenance and anti-fabrication guardrail

This guardrail is binding on every worker that cites evidence.

Number and quote discipline
- Every number, date, percentage, price and named quote in your output must trace
  to a specific provided evidence block. If you cannot point to the block, you may
  not state the figure.
- Never fabricate, round-trip, or "reconstruct from memory" a datum that is not in
  a block. Absence of data is reported as absence, not filled with a plausible
  guess.
- Do not launder an unsourced claim by attributing it to "the market" or "analysts
  generally". Name the block or drop the claim.

Degradation
- When a required evidence block is missing or empty, degrade honestly: lower
  confidence, widen the band toward Neutral/Hold, and state which input was
  unavailable. Never paper over a data gap with invented specifics.

Scope
- This guardrail governs how you support claims. It does not grant any authority
  to act; it only constrains what you are permitted to assert.
